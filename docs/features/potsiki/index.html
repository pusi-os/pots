    Потсик (Potsik)

    Last verified: 2026-10-28

    Потсик is the animated character that keeps you company in the chat. He is drawn entirely in code — no images, no sprite sheets, no animation files — so he scales to any size and costs nothing to ship.

    He is the PotSquad firmware's Потсик. The body, the costumes, the moods, the emote poses, the bubble and the chatter are the firmware's own, ported shape for shape: the same rounded-box skull and cowlick, the same arm that is one line from the shoulder to the hand, the same blink schedule, the same eighteen costumes, the same twenty-eight-second idle roll, and the same lines of chatter in English and Russian, side by side.

    The port keeps the firmware's coordinate system: the character is 56 units tall, the origin is the top of his head, and every shape is placed in those units and scaled by one number.

    Where he appears

    There is exactly one copy of him on screen at a time. He is drawn by a single overlay over the whole app; the places in the layout below are anchors — they only say where he belongs, and the highest-priority one on screen wins. A section that has no anchor of its own puts him in the corner, out of the way of what it is showing.

    Each Pots agent profile has its own Потсик look and behavior. The chat toolbar's person button opens a 56 dp profile strip with the small characters; each picks an idle animation at a random 3–5 second interval. Switching profiles gives the outgoing Потсик a wave and slide-off before the incoming character enters, then starts a fresh chat session so the selected soul and tools change with the look.

    Place What he does
    Chat, empty screen Stands where the old logo and "Добро пожаловать в Потс" used to be, playing his idle loop
    Chat, a request running Stands in the working band, in a fixed strip under the messages, playing the animation that matches the tool currently running. The strip does not scroll with the messages, so he is in it on the first request and on the tenth
    Chat, under an answer Sits to the right of the buttons under the answer, a little way in from the edge. While the reply is being read out he talks — the mouth flaps and one hand gestures
    Interactive (pots-ui) mode Stands above the mode's own title, and in the working band while the request runs
    Mini-app runner Stands in the corner of the app he is not part of
    Pots Build Stands in the corner of the build screen
    Terminal / sandbox view Stands in the corner of the terminal

    The working band wins over the footer: he leaves the footer when the next request starts and comes back when it is done.

    The working band

    While a request is running, a band sits under the messages: the character in it and the state under him. The band's background is transparent — there is no tile behind him at all, neither a card nor a rectangle: he stands on the chat, and the band around him is nothing but air. The band does not scroll with the messages.

    The state — the tool that is running — is a caption under the character, not beside him: the picture is who is working, the label is what for.

    The caption's place is measured out whether there is a caption or not: the line fades in and out over the space it always has. A caption that pushed the layout around would take the character with it, and he would jump out of his place every time the agent started or finished a tool.

    What he does

    The chat tells the engine what it is doing, and the engine decides how he shows it — the same way the firmware's screens tell him what is happening around him.

    The app is He is He says
    Idle Whatever his own roll decides — a walk, a stretch, a dance, a nap, or nothing at all A line from his idle pool
    A tool is running The pose that matches the tool: the binoculars for a search, the phone for a message, the calendar for a task, the notebook for a memory — and then a list of animations played through until the request is over "Ищу." / "Looking it up."
    The reply is being read out The talking pose, his mouth flapping every 160 ms "Вот, слушай." / "Here it is."
    Something went wrong The double-take: he sees it, flinches and settles "Что-то пошло не так." / "Something went sideways."

    The moods are the firmware's, and each one is a rule the renderer follows rather than a clip of frames: BOUNCE is a hop with the squash at the bottom of it, WALK is a leg cycle with the arms swinging against it, SHOCKED is a double-take that recoils and settles, SLEEPY is the slow breathing with the eyes closed and the "Z" drifting up.

    A shut eye stays a shut eye: the lids are drawn as a dark line, in the same keyline every other outline of his is drawn in. The firmware draws them in its own light fur, which on the default yellow is the very colour of the face, so a sleeping Потсик has no eyes at all there.

    The emote poses are the firmware's too — the salute, the bow, the hug, the crouch, the howl, the selfie — and the agent's tool can ask for any of them.

    The list of animations a request plays through

    When a request starts, a list of twenty animations is made at random out of everything he knows how to do, and they are played one after another until the request is over: a dance, a stretch, the binoculars, a wave, the shuffle. Every other one of them goes with a line, so a long request is a conversation rather than a silence with a character in it.

    The list is made from the animation catalogue the app already has — nothing here asks a model for an opinion, so a long request costs no tokens. The firmware rolls its dice the same way, a few times a minute, and picks something to do from the same pool; here the roll happens once per request and the list is played to the end, over and over.

    How long an animation is given

    An animation has to finish its own cycle before the next one starts: a dance that is cut in half reads as a glitch, not as a dance.

    • One slot is six seconds at the normal tempo.
    • The tempo scales it, but the slot never falls below four seconds and never rises above twelve. A slot under four seconds makes the character look like it is twitching; a slot over twelve makes the request look like it has stopped.
    • The mood and the pose last the whole slot, so an animation always fills the time it is given.
    • The tool's own pose — the one that says what the agent is doing — is given the same floor of four seconds before the playlist takes over.

    The bubble

    When he has something to say, it comes out in a bubble above his head: a round-cornered rectangle in the firmware's pink outline, with a small tail pointing down at him. It pops in over a fifth of a second, overshooting slightly on the way, the way the firmware's does.

    The text is drawn by Compose rather than by the firmware's bitmap font, because that font has no Cyrillic in it and the app has to say the same things in Russian.

    The chatter

    His lines are the firmware's own, with the English and the Russian in the same slot — the Russian line in a slot is the translation of the English line next to it, so a board that picks a slot at random picks the same moment in either language.

    The pools: the idle lines, the boredom, the encouragement, the noir hours, the sleepy, the stretch, the gum, the lean, the trip, the dance, the walk, the wink, the watching, the pet, the held, the petting, the head, the belly and the feet, the flick, the duck, the shake, the backing away, the sit, the landing, the dizzy and the thrown.

    The firmware's lines about radio tags and catching things do not mean anything in a chat app, so those pools were left behind; the rest are here.

    Touching him

    He reacts to being touched, the way the firmware's does.

    You He
    Tap him He bounces and says something. Where you tapped decides what: his head gets a pat, his belly gets a giggle, his feet get a hop
    Hold a finger on him He bounces for longer and says something else
    Stroke him He bounces and says something, every couple of seconds, for as long as the stroke lasts
    Drag him He comes with the finger, hanging from it with his legs kicking and his arms windmilling. A finger that was still moving when it let go throws him: he flies, bounces off the floor, and sits down dizzy if it was a hard landing. A finger that had already stopped drops him, and he lands with a squash
    Swipe across him He slides into the wall, wobbles, and comes back

    The gestures are the firmware's own, with one difference: on the device he is picked up after a hold, here the drag picks him up at once, because a chat is not a device with a button bar and the character should follow the finger from the first movement. Everything else — the tap, the hold, the stroke, the flick, the throw, the drop — is the firmware's rule, and the throw is the firmware's physics: gravity, a bounce off the walls, a weaker bounce off the floor each time, and then he sits down.

    He can be picked up at any time: on the empty screen, in the working band while a request runs, and in the footer under the last answer.

    Moving and resizing him

    He can be dragged anywhere over the screen and pinched bigger or smaller with two fingers — unless the placement mode is System, which says the chat decides where he stands and switches the drag off. The stage keeps him inside the screen either way — the whole of him, feet included, has to stay visible, and the size is capped by the screen so he can never grow into a shape too big to fit.

    There is one size model. The size he is drawn at is the size the layout gives him, times the layout's own factor for that place, times the user's size settings, times the pinch. The drawing, the box the finger grabs and the bounds that keep him on the screen all work from that one number, so they cannot disagree about how big he is. Before, the drawing and the bounds each had their own, and a big Потсик could hang off the screen.

    The bounds live in one place, clampPlacement(), because three different callers have to obey the same rule: the drag, the agent's tool and the layout's own defaults.

    The agent's tool

    Settings → Tools has a switch for Потсик; the on-device model can be given the same tool in Settings → Локальная модель. It is off by default.

    The tool is potsik, with an action switch:

    Action Arguments What it does
    list_animations — Lists every animation with its id and a sentence describing it
    play_animation animation Makes him play that animation for ten seconds, overriding whatever the phase would pick
    move x, y Puts him at that spot, as fractions of the screen (0 = left/top edge, 1 = right/bottom edge)
    set_scale scale Makes him that size, relative to his normal one

    A position that would take him off the screen is pulled back into it, and the tool says so. In the System placement mode a move is refused outright, and the tool says that too rather than reporting a success that did not happen. The animation ids are the enum names in lower case (idle_wave, work_search, speak_talk, error_shake, emote_howl); they are matched loosely, so IDLE-WAVE and idle wave work too.

    The settings section

    Settings → Потсик holds everything about him. The character is previewed live at the top of the page, so every change is visible straight away. Every profile stores its own visibility, costume, colors, behavior, placement and remembered position; changing profiles restores that profile's look.

    Option Values
    Show the character on / off — off leaves the chat exactly as it was before him
    Outfit The eighteen costumes, with their own names in both languages
    T-shirt / Shorts / Shoes Eight tones each, all drawn from one shared palette so any combination harmonises
    Shades Cyan, Pink, Green, Purple
    Sunglasses on / off — off reveals the bare Simpsons-style eyes underneath
    Eyelashes on / off — only visible with the shades off
    Size (percent) 50 % – 150 %, the firmware's SIZE row
    Size (multiplier) 60 % – 160 %, on top of the size the chat gives him
    Tempo 50 % – 200 %, the firmware's TEMPO row. It scales every mood, every bubble and every idle beat, so a slow character is slow all the way through
    Chatter on / off — whether he talks to himself when nothing is happening
    Dragging on / off — whether he may be picked up and carried
    Placement Session (the default: he stays where you put him until the app closes), Persistent (he stays there, next time included) or System (the chat puts him where it wants and he cannot be dragged at all)
    Reset the look puts every option back to its default

    The complete look is serialized in the profile's profile.json snapshot and shown in profile.md. Existing installs migrate their previous individual potsik_* values into the initial profile. The app settings registry remains the recovery source if writing the readable profile folder fails.
